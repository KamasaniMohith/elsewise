# Elsewise — Product Definition

## 1. What is Elsewise?

Elsewise is a cognitive and knowledge playground designed to make spare minutes mentally meaningful.

Instead of automatically filling short periods of free time with passive scrolling, Elsewise provides small experiences that encourage active thinking, curiosity, learning, and mental reset.

---

## 2. Core Pillars

### Challenge

Short activities that require active thinking.

Examples may include:

- Logic
- Memory
- Attention
- Pattern recognition
- Reasoning
- Problem solving

### Discover

Experiences that encourage curiosity and learning.

Examples may include:

- Interesting facts
- Science
- Technology
- History
- Ideas
- Questions worth exploring

### Reset

Short experiences intended to help the user pause and mentally reset.

These should not make unsupported medical or therapeutic claims.

---

## 3. Core Philosophy

Elsewise should make the user's spare time intentional rather than automatically passive.

The product should prioritize:

- Active engagement
- Curiosity
- Scientific honesty
- Short sessions
- Simple interactions
- Reusability
- Personal progress without unhealthy pressure

---

## 4. Scientific Honesty

Elsewise must distinguish between:

1. Performance on a specific task
2. Improvement through practice on similar tasks
3. Broader real-world cognitive benefits

The product must not make unsupported claims that a game can:

- Increase general intelligence
- Prevent or treat medical conditions
- Permanently improve cognition
- Replace professional medical or psychological care

Evidence should be presented carefully and with appropriate context.

---

## 5. Version 0.0 Goal

Version 0.0 is the foundation of Elsewise.

The objective is not to build the complete product immediately.

The objective is to establish:

- Repository structure
- Development environment
- Product definition
- Technical architecture
- Design system foundation
- Initial application shell
- First working experience

---

## 6. Initial Success Criteria

Elsewise v0.0 should eventually be able to:

- Start locally
- Display the Elsewise identity
- Provide a clear navigation structure
- Present at least one working cognitive experience
- Record basic session information locally
- Maintain a clean and reproducible development setup

---

## 7. Product Principles

### Free-first

The core experience should remain accessible without requiring payment.

### Short by design

Experiences should work well within small amounts of available time.

### No manipulation

The product should not depend on addictive engagement loops or artificial urgency.

### Evidence-aware

Claims about cognition should be supported by credible evidence and communicated with appropriate limitations.

### Privacy-conscious

Collect only information that is necessary for the product.

### Build progressively

Every version should have a clear purpose and avoid unnecessary complexity.
